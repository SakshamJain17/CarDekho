## Performance edition original artwork

`performance-hero.png` was generated using the built-in image-generation tool,
then copied into this repository. It is original fictional concept artwork, not
a Lamborghini photograph, a configured listing or a model-output visualization.
No affiliation with Lamborghini is implied. The other websites' artwork is
preserved unchanged.

Generation prompt: Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: original photorealistic
automotive website hero, wide landscape 16:9. Create a premium editorial
photograph of a fictional unbranded angular graphite-black mid-engine sports
coupe with restrained electric-yellow detailing, NOT an existing automaker
model. Entire car on the right two-thirds of the frame, dramatic low front
three-quarter view, front facing left toward camera, strong crisp geometric
bodywork, precision wheels, narrow original LED headlights. Massive brutalist
concrete tunnel opens onto a sunlit rugged mountain landscape at blue hour;
very dark negative space on the left third for white website headings; clean
architectural shadows and a thin line of golden light. Premium realistic
material finish, carbon details, finely controlled reflections, believable
automotive geometry, cinematic photography, absolutely no branding, no badges,
no words, no numbers, no watermark, no people. Landscape composition suitable
for full-width luxury automotive interface.
