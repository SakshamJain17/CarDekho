# Visual asset credits

## Original hero artwork

`grand-tourer-hero.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. It is fictional concept artwork, not a Ferrari photograph, a listed vehicle, or evidence about a price prediction. No affiliation with Ferrari or CarDekho is implied.

Prompt: Original premium automotive editorial photograph, landscape 16:9; an unbranded deep racing-red grand touring coupe in a dark concrete studio opening onto a mountain horizon at blue hour; low front three-quarter view; whole car on the right with dark negative space on the left; wet graphite floor, softbox paint reflections, warm edge lighting and cool dusk; photorealistic materials; no lettering, brand logos, UI, or watermark.
